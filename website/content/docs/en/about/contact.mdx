Questions, ideas, or partnership inquiries — any of the channels below will reach us.

## Bugs & feature requests

- **GitHub Issues** (preferred): [github.com/Alndaly/OpenStudio/issues](https://github.com/Alndaly/OpenStudio/issues) — reproduction steps plus the relevant `publisher.log` / `backend.log` snippets speed things up a lot.
- **GitHub Discussions**: usage questions and open-ended ideas — [start a discussion](https://github.com/Alndaly/OpenStudio/discussions).
