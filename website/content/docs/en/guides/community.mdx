The website's [community](/community) holds publicly shared **workflows** and **boards**, alongside three market pages for
[plugins](/plugins), [workflows](/workflows) and [boards](/boards). Each share is a `.json` project file: a workflow is a set of wired
nodes, a board holds its media, positions and links; open it, swap in your own models and media, and carry on. Shared files **don't**
include account keys or private media.

## Find one

The community page filters by kind (workflow / board), sorts by newest or popular, and searches. A share's page shows:

- a **preview**: the workflow's node graph or the board's layout — **View in canvas** lets you pan around the whole thing;
- **What is this**, file size, **license**, publish date and the author (click the name for their creator page and other public shares);
- download, like and favorite counts, and comments. Liking, favoriting and commenting need a MosaelCloud sign-in.

## Import into Mosael

- **Workflows**: choose **Open in Mosael**. The desktop app shows **Import a community share** with the author, kind, size and download
  count; nothing is imported until you choose **Import into workspace**, and it then confirms the name. This goes through a
  `mosael://import?share=…` link, so the desktop app needs to be installed.
- **Boards** can't be imported in one click yet. **Download** the `.json`, then use **Import** on the Boards page.
- You can also download first and use **Import workflow** on the Workflows page.

Then open it: pick your own provider and model for model nodes, fill the start node's required inputs, and run once the readiness
check passes (see [Workflows](/docs/guides/workflows)). If a share can't be read, it says which case it is: the link is wrong or the
share was deleted, MosaelCloud is unreachable (check your network and proxy), or the file isn't a usable workflow.

## Share your work

1. Export from Mosael: **Export as file** on the Workflows page, **Export** on the Boards page — you get a `.json`.
2. Sign in on the website and choose **Share your work** in the page header. The first time, pick a **display name** (and an optional
   **handle**); it appears next to your shares and comments and can be changed any time.
3. Choose the kind, upload the `.json`, write a clear **title, summary and license** — that's how people tell whether it's what they
   need — and **Publish**.

It then shows up in the community and on your creator page; the **My shares** area of your website console manages what you've published.
