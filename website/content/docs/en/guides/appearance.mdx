Open **Settings → Appearance** in the sidebar. Changes apply across the interface immediately and are saved on this device; no restart is needed.

![Appearance settings: theme, background and fonts](/media/screens/en/settings-appearance.png)

## Theme and background

Choose light, dark or system theme. With a custom background enabled, Media, AI Studio, Plugins, Scheduled tasks, Edit and Settings share the background's visual layers. Floating forms, menus and assistant panels use translucent blurred surfaces to keep text readable.

## Choose a font

Each font card previews Chinese, English and numbers. Options include default Inter, the system font, Noto Sans SC, Noto Serif SC and LXGW WenKai, plus four combinations with more distinctive Latin lettering:

| English font | Chinese companion | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space Grotesk | Noto Sans SC | Geometric and clean |
| Newsreader | Noto Serif SC | Editorial serif |
| Caveat | LXGW WenKai | Flowing handwriting |
| Kalam | LXGW WenKai | Handwritten notes |

Select a card to preview the entire interface. Bundled fonts work without an online font service; system fonts vary by device. Interface fonts do not change media, subtitle styling or fonts in exported video.
