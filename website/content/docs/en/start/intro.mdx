Mosael is a **local-first desktop workspace for creative work**. Media, documents, boards, 3D scenes and editing projects share a workspace, with agents and workflows available to help move work between them.

## From research to a finished piece

- **Collect material**: keep footage, images and sound in the [media library](/docs/guides/media); import PDFs, Word files, slides and spreadsheets as [documents](/docs/guides/documents) that are parsed into readable text on your machine; keep scripts, transcripts and research in [notes](/docs/guides/notes); and save recurring characters, locations and props as named entries in the [asset library](/docs/guides/assets).
- **Plan visually**: connect documents, references, assets, 3D scenes and generation items on a [creative board](/docs/guides/boards). Items do their own work — transcribe, translate, denoise, split a grid — and a timeline item gives you a rough cut right on the board. Comments and markers keep the discussion anchored.
- **Rehearse shots**: arrange space and lighting in [3D scenes](/docs/guides/scenes), animate cameras and objects, then use the resulting shots as image or video references.
- **Make the edit**: talk to the agent and generate images, video and music in [AI Studio](/docs/guides/ai-studio); make a face speak or re-sync lips with [digital humans](/docs/guides/digital-humans); then [edit](/docs/guides/editing) with timelines, transcripts, captions, voiceover, color and export.
- **Repeat and publish**: reuse steps with [workflows](/docs/guides/workflows) (official templates cover finished videos, translated dubbing, product shorts and social analytics), arrange [scheduled runs](/docs/guides/scheduler), and track uploads in [Publish](/docs/guides/publishing).
- **Extend and manage**: [plugins](/docs/guides/plugins) bring in ComfyUI, object storage, document parsing and more; on a shared deployment, the deployment admin installs engines and manages members and costs in the [admin console](/docs/guides/admin).

![Studio: recent edits, all projects and project search](/media/screens/en/home.png)

Create a project on **Studio**, the home page (you name it first and it stays there), then click anywhere on its card or row to open it in the editor; **Select** lets you delete projects in bulk. Media, Assets, Notes, 3D Scenes, boards and the other pages live in the left sidebar; task, publishing and AI cost summaries are in **Statistics**.

## Data and network access

Projects and media are stored on your computer by default. Organizing local media, editing and exporting can work offline. Cloud models, URL downloads, online tools and platform publishing need a network connection. Local transcription and voice cloning require their model files to be downloaded first.

When connected to a team backend, shared data lives on that server. Browser sign-in profiles remain on the desktop device that runs uploads and automation. See [remote deployment](/docs/guides/remote) and [browser profiles](/docs/guides/browser-pool).

## Where to start

[Install the app](/docs/start/download), then follow the [quick start](/docs/start/quickstart) to make an export. [Connect a model service](/docs/guides/providers) when you need AI. The documentation follows this sequence: Get started → Organize & plan → Produce & edit → Automate & publish → Extend & deploy. On a phone, the compact navigation bar opens either the full documentation or this page's headings.
