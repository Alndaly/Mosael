Mosael is a **local-first desktop workspace for creative work**. Media, documents, boards, 3D scenes and editing projects share a workspace, with agents and workflows available to help move work between them.

## From research to a finished piece

- **Collect material**: keep footage, images and sound in the [media library](/docs/guides/media), and scripts, transcripts and research in [notes and documents](/docs/guides/notes).
- **Plan visually**: connect documents, references and generation nodes on a [creative board](/docs/guides/boards). Use comments for discussion and markers to return to important areas.
- **Rehearse shots**: arrange space and lighting in [3D scenes](/docs/guides/scenes), animate cameras and objects, then use the resulting shots as image or video references.
- **Make the edit**: use [AI Studio](/docs/guides/ai-studio) to generate media and work on projects, then [edit](/docs/guides/editing) with timelines, transcripts, captions, color and sound.
- **Repeat and publish**: reuse steps with [workflows](/docs/guides/workflows), arrange [scheduled runs](/docs/guides/scheduler), and track uploads in [Publish](/docs/guides/publishing).

Create or open a project from Home to enter the editor. Open notes, boards and 3D scenes from the sidebar. Task and cost summaries are available in **Statistics**.

## Data and network access

Projects and media are stored on your computer by default. Organizing local media, editing and exporting can work offline. Cloud models, URL downloads, online tools and platform publishing need a network connection. Local transcription and voice cloning require their model files to be downloaded first.

When connected to a team backend, shared data lives on that server. Browser sign-in profiles remain on the desktop device that runs uploads and automation. See [remote deployment](/docs/guides/remote) and [browser profiles](/docs/guides/browser-pool).

## Where to start

[Install the app](/docs/start/download), then follow the [quick start](/docs/start/quickstart) to make an export. [Connect a model service](/docs/guides/providers) when you need AI. The documentation follows this sequence: Get started → Organize & plan → Produce & edit → Automate & publish → Extend & deploy. On a phone, the compact navigation bar opens either the full documentation or this page's headings.
